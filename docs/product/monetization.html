Why this converts

  • Your-question funnel: real panel prompts feel valuable immediately.
  • Per-topic custom quota: natural upgrade after 2 free custom feedbacks.
  • Voice differentiator: Content / Clarity / Delivery beats text-only sites.
  • Pack gating: full role packs + long mocks sit behind Pro.
