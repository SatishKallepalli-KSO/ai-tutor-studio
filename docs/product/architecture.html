System architecture

Entitlements

  • Learn: all tracks + video paths always readable; mark Reviewed manually.
  • Free practice: starter language tracks · 5 bank feedbacks / UTC day · 2 custom AI feedbacks / topic · 15-min timed mock.
  • Pro: all tracks · unlimited bank + custom · full role packs & timed mocks · Stripe portal.
  • Social + jobs: available on Free and Pro (Hire launchpad — For companies / Hiring teams).
  • Admin: ADMIN_EMAILS → /admin.

UI architecture (current)

  • Editorial dark theme: Fraunces + Sora · jade + champagne accents.
  • Primary nav: Home · Practice · Your question · Agentic AI (+ More).
  • Slim launchpads: hero → doors → collapsed catalog.
  • Pages-only mode: demo jobs/profiles with local fallbacks.

Security talking points

  • bcrypt passwords · JWT bearer · Stripe secrets server-side only.
  • Feedback, checkout, messaging, and job posts require auth where gated.
  • Private profiles hidden from public talent browse.
  • Chat only between accepted connections.
  • Voice stays in-browser (Web Speech API) by default.
