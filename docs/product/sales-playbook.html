Problem (say this)

  • Candidates over-read and under-speak — then freeze in real interviews.
  • LeetCode trains puzzles, not ownership / people narratives out loud.
  • ChatGPT has no curriculum, quota, or productized coaching loop.

Solution (say this)

  • Learn topic docs — mark Reviewed when ready (manual).
  • Practice your own question or bank / role-pack drills into the mic.
  • Get AI feedback — Content / Clarity / Delivery + before/after replay.
  • Level up — weak-spot coach, timed mock scorecard, shareable link.

Objection handling

“Speech-to-text isn’t perfect.”

Transcript is editable; coaching still grades structure and fillers.

“Why only 2 custom feedbacks on free?”

Enough to feel the loop on a real prompt. Bank practice + 15-min mock stay available; Pro unlocks unlimited custom and full role-pack mocks.

“What about hiring / LinkedIn features?”

For companies / Hiring teams exist at /for-companies. Lead with learn + oral coaching.
