Sales playbook

Use this page on calls, in proposals, and as a leave-behind. Pair with the live app and the architecture / flows docs.

One-liner

AI Tutor Studio helps engineers practice for real interviews — study curated topics, answer out loud, and get AI coaching on content and spoken delivery — with a Free tier and Pro unlock for Staff/EM and unlimited feedback.

Problem

  • Candidates over-prepare by reading and under-prepare by speaking.
  • Generic chatbots don’t structure curriculum or monetize cleanly.
  • Interview loops (Staff/EM) need narrative + system design + AI depth.

Solution

  • One-stop shop: docs → practice → feedback per topic.
  • Voice-native: mic answers + delivery coaching.
  • Freemium commerce: Stripe subscriptions like HackerRank-style products.
  • Works offline-ish: local rubric when LLM key isn’t set.

Ideal customers

B2C

Job seekers (Senior/Staff/EM), career switchers into AI.

B2B (next)

Bootcamps, internal L&D, recruiting academies wanting branded tracks.

5-minute demo script

  1. Open tracks — show Free vs Pro badges.
  2. Pick Python or HTML — open Learn docs (key points + example).
  3. Practice — click Speak answer, dictate 60–90s, submit.
  4. Show coaching — score, gaps, Spoken delivery & grammar.
  5. Open Staff track — hit paywall → Pricing → monthly/yearly.
  6. Close — “Same loop for EM and Java→AI; Pro unlocks the career tracks.”

Competitive wedges

Alternative Their gap Our wedge
LeetCode / HackerRank Coding puzzles ≠ behavioral/system oral Voice + narrative + Staff/EM tracks
ChatGPT alone No curriculum, quota, or billing product Structured tracks + freemium + Stripe
Coaching services Expensive, not always-on Always-on practice with upgrade path

Objection handling

“Speech-to-text isn’t perfect.”

Transcript is editable; coaching still grades structure and fillers. Same as real interviews — clarity matters.

“Do I need OpenAI?”

No — local rubric ships. OpenAI upgrades coaching quality for Pro hosts.

“Can we white-label?”

API + curriculum files are separable; Docker deploy supports a single branded service. Seats/SSO are the natural enterprise upsell.

Pricing talk track

  • Free forever for learning + starter practice.
  • Pro at $19/mo or $149/yr (~35% save) for interview tracks + unlimited coaching.
  • Cheaper than one human mock session; available daily.
