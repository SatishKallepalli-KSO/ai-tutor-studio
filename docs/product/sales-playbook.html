ICP (who buys now)

Primary · B2C Senior → Staff / EM candidates

Already strong technically; weak on oral narrative and delivery under panel pressure. Will pay $19/mo for unlimited coaching.

Problem (say this)

  • Candidates over-read and under-speak — then freeze in Staff/EM rooms.
  • LeetCode trains puzzles, not ownership / people narratives out loud.
  • ChatGPT has no curriculum, quota, or productized coaching loop.

Solution (say this)

  • Study Staff / EM topic docs.
  • Speak answers into the mic (or type).
  • Coach — score, gaps, stronger answer, delivery tips.
  • Upgrade — Pro unlocks full tracks + unlimited feedback.

Objection handling

“Speech-to-text isn’t perfect.”

Transcript is editable; coaching still grades structure and fillers.

“Staff is Pro-only — why try free?”

Docs are free on every path. Starter language tracks + daily feedback prove the loop; Pro unlocks Staff/EM practice depth.

“What about hiring / LinkedIn features?”

They exist for later B2B. Today we sell practice. Hire is the upsell.

Pricing talk track

  • Free: learn docs + starter practice + 5 feedbacks/day.
  • Pro $19/mo or $149/yr: Staff, EM, advanced tracks + unlimited coaching.
  • Hiring pilots: only after practice traction — talk to sales.

Channels that attract this ICP
