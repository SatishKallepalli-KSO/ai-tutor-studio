One-liner

AI Tutor Studio is a talent studio — Learn. Practice. Hire. Candidates study and practice out loud with AI coaching; companies post jobs, browse talent profiles, and connect — Free + Pro with Stripe.

Problem

  • Candidates read more than they speak — then freeze in oral loops.
  • Hiring teams lack one place for readiness + structured job posts.
  • Generic chatbots don’t ship curriculum, entitlements, or a hire surface.

Solution

  • Learn → Practice → Coach on every topic.
  • Voice-native mic answers + delivery coaching.
  • Hire layer: jobs, profiles, connections, chat.
  • Freemium commerce: Stripe Pro for Staff/EM depth.

Ideal customers

B2C Talent

Senior/Staff/EM seekers, Java→AI and Snowflake career switchers.

B2B Hiring teams

Recruiters and eng managers who want practice-ready talent + job posts in one studio. Bootcamps / L&D next.

6-minute demo script

  1. Learner mode — open Learn, show Free vs Pro tracks.
  2. Practice — Speak answer 60–90s → coaching panel.
  3. Profile — open-to-work talent card.
  4. Network + Chat — connect → message.
  5. Switch to Recruiter — post a job on the board.
  6. Close — Pricing for Staff/EM unlimited coaching; hiring pilot CTA.

Competitive wedges

Alternative Gap Our wedge

ChatGPT alone No curriculum or billing Tracks + Free/Pro + Stripe

Objection handling

“Speech-to-text isn’t perfect.”

Transcript is editable; coaching still grades structure and fillers.

“Do I need OpenAI?”

No — local rubric ships. OpenAI upgrades Pro coaching quality.

“Is hiring ready?”

Jobs, profiles, connections, and chat ship today; cloud API persists across users. Interview scorecards are the natural next B2B upsell.

Pricing talk track

  • Free: learn + starter practice + social/jobs.
  • Pro: $19/mo or $149/yr for interview tracks + unlimited coaching.
  • Hiring pilots: talk to sales for multi-seat / branded loops.
