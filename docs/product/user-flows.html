Experience design

User flows

Learner journey: slim launchpad → Practice hub or Your question → AI feedback (Content / Clarity / Delivery). Hire launchpad for companies is secondary.

1) Learn home launchpad

flowchart TD A[Land Home] --> B[Hero] B --> C[Doors] C --> D[Practice hub] C --> E[Your question] C --> F[Agentic AI] C --> G[Collapsed catalog] G --> H[Topic docs] H --> I[Mark as reviewed] I --> J[Practice this topic]

Learn progress is Reviewed / Mark as reviewed — not auto-marked on click.

2) Practice your own question (flagship)

flowchart TD A[Your question] --> B[Paste or type prompt] B --> C[Speak or type answer] C --> D[AI feedback Content Clarity Delivery] D --> E{Free custom quota} E -->|Under 2/topic| F[Cloud sync if signed in] E -->|Hit limit| G[Upgrade to Pro] D --> H[Before/after replay]

3) Practice hub — packs · mock · weak spots

flowchart TD A[Practice hub] --> B[Role pack Staff EM AI engineer] A --> C[Timed mock] A --> D[Weak-spot coach] A --> E[Bank drills] B --> F[Speak answers] C --> F D --> F E --> F F --> G[End scorecard] G --> H[Share /scorecard/id]

Free includes a short 15-minute mock; full timed packs are Pro.

4) Agentic AI path

flowchart TD A[Agentic AI] --> B[YouTube watch resume] B --> C[Per-lesson progress] C --> D{Phase done?} D -->|Yes| E[Practice with AI feedback] D -->|Keep watching| B E --> F[Content Clarity Delivery]

5) Hire launchpad

flowchart TD A[Hire / Jobs] --> B[Hero doors] B --> C[Jobs board] B --> D[For companies] B --> E[Talent / Network] D --> F[Hiring teams pitch]

No “phase 2” naming — For companies / Hiring teams.

6) Paywall

flowchart TD A[Practice / feedback] --> B{Signed in?} B -->|No| C[Register] B -->|Yes| D{Pro track or pack?} D -->|Locked| E[Pricing] D -->|Free track| F{Bank or custom quota?} F -->|Empty| E F -->|OK| G[Coaching] E --> H{Stripe?} H -->|Yes| I[Checkout] H -->|No| J[Demo upgrade] I --> G J --> G
